Winter 2027 schedule planner

Browse MBA, SWELL and non-MBA classes, add the ones you want, and the planner flags conflicts and shows your week.

How this works

1. Pick your MBA classes first with Add. Then open SWELL classes to see only the SWELL activities that fit around them. The bar under this header always shows your totals.

2. Sections tagged Conflicts with 1st year core classes overlap the 1st year core schedule (Tue/Thu 8:00–9:15, 9:30–10:45 and 12:30–1:45).

3. Tap ☆ to save a class to Favorites. The ⋯ button on a class has Class already taken and Not interested in this class. Hidden classes go to the Not for me tab, and a message lets you undo right away.

4. For non-MBA classes, use Set my time after checking your section on BYU MyMap, and they will count in your week and conflict checks.

5. Everything is saved in this browser. Use the backup buttons at the bottom of the page to keep or move your picks.
